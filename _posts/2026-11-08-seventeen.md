# the seventeenth pole is still obscured 

i feel like a retard alluding to it like that.

its my birthday today and i dont feel any older, i dont feel any better, i only feel worse

since my last post two days ago, detailing what i thought about my future, a lot has changed. i cant see it as a straight line anymore, there are still wires and poles but there are paths, branching paths with different glimpses of the future. the one straight infront of me is blank, as if saying if i dont do anything now,  i will become nothing. it makes sense. the path just to the right is a hole with a big wall behind it. there are things blocking this hole, but they are all so small to me right now. the left is a narrow path, but everything i detailed before is still there.

ive always found it harder when at forking paths to go left, right feels most natural, after that is straight and then left. you walk on the righthand side, your right hand is your dominant one, would it be right to go right for something so final

i still feel like a retard for these allusions

i have been getting worse but i have this feeling of helplessness, where i know i can help myself, where i know i can seek the help i need, that there are people who care about me, but i dont want to seek this help. i am fine with the path i am on now.

i have been shutting myself in from my friends, i cant stand to talk to them for too long, they are just rude and so full of themselves that they cannot see other people, ive brought up when jokes are not funny, i feel ive been clear with expressing my emotions while around them and still they dont seem to know anything

i havent outright said that i am upset or sad, i just feel it shows on my face, my gait, how i speak my words and walk my walk, i feel stupid for thinking that they could pick up on this, these kids are retards and i feel they could care less, or if anything were to be brought up, they would be too scared or worried to delve deeper into that

# i have been getting worse

i dont care for those allusions anymore

thats my word of the day allusion - like the 6th was prospect

ive felt my attitude on eating really slope downwards, i have been eating less and when i do it is too much. i cant tell if its just because im sick but ive been vomiting a lot too, im incredibly unhappy with how i look and nothing that i can do will be enough. ive gotten into the habit of weighing myself most every day and the days that i eat that number goes up by a few pounds, just for the day. i hate it. ive aformentioned that if i saw myself above 120lbs i would seriously consider killing myself but that number has dropped to 110, i weighed in at 107 the other week and ive chased that high for too long. i hate stepping on the scale and seeing it adjust past 110, even just barely the other day at 111.8, i felt this overwhelming dread.

i dont want to acknowledge that this attitude on eating could be something bigger like a disorder, but the fact that ive felt this way for this long and how its amplified this much i feel i can say that now. i really dont want to.

the other day i was thinking about working over the summer, the things i did and the people i met. i recalled how i had taken the marker, the drawings, my gloves and my vest home with me. i recalled how i had taken the boxcutter accientally as it was attached to the vest as well and i had forgotten to take it off. when i was working there in display, i would have to open boxes in display a lot, which meant a lot of accidental cuts. whenever i would notice these, shallow, faint cuts, id stare for a minute. 

i didnt know what to think of them

i had thought to myself that it had sucked and it had hurt, but a small part of me was very interested about how it looked. i dont know why, there was no feeling of 'needing' to do it for some reason to be hurt, it just looked. after that over my time there i sought it more purposefully, again, no need to be hurt, just the thought of a mark and a weird sensitive feeling. 

i dug the vest out of the closet a few days ago, the box cutter came with it. i looked it over for the first time in months, and really really looked. i had only ever used this single blade, but it came with two, double sided, which, i should have known it would be. the side i had been opening boxes with for for months was dull and almost smooth, the opposite side almost felt the same, but it was clearly different as it could actually cut skin. there were different settings that controlled the blade length. the smallest one is far too large even still to be cutting into myself with, so i dont usually do it that way.

ive cut deep enough for like a normal cut that you would get elsewhere instead of the shallower beading things i would do at work, couldnt really do more than that there. ive reflected on these cuts after ive done them, stared at my arm and ran my thumb over them. i hate that feeling, i dont know if its too much to describe but how like when you get a scratch and there are those little pieces of like skin that are still attached or when you get like a hangnail but i could run my thumb over it and it would like feel like running your thumb over that. maybe im doing it wrong

suicide has been a frequent thought in my mind as well. thats what the hole is on the right path. i havent acted on these thoughts, but ive thought that if something were to come up i would be okay with it. that if in the moment i got an insane spike of dread and despair that i would be okay with dying there. i was biking home on friday, going through an intersection, i could see the oncoming cars were filtering out into an open path, and there was a car starting to turn straight into my path and i sped up. i know that if even the car had hit me, the driver would have some sense to stop. that at that speed nothing would even break and i would be simply knocked off my bike onto the road. i dont wear a helmet, so maybe if i had been hit hard enough to slam my head into the pavement that could be enough but i seriously doubt that. i could only imagine if that happened and i had lived and gone to the hospital to get my head stitched up my mom would lecture me about wearing a helmet and that i would never hear the end of it.

there is this bridge i biked past on the same day that overlooks the highway that i have thought of a lot as well. that could be instant if i land the right way and if i dont surely a car would finish it. the other day i was walking past the school and somehow the topic of conversation was how if you had fallen off, you would survive. i looked up and yeah, sure. its 3 floors, and if you jumped and really braced yourself im sure that you could only break your legs and survive, but if you had really been purposeful and set on it, you could die.

that got me thinking last night, how if i were to die so young, so close to my birthday, would i become something of an example? i hate that feeling. i would only want my family and friends to think about me, if i was turned into some 'act' or was talked about in the media that they would get things wrong about me, how i feel and act and what drove me to a decision. i want to be somewhat forgotten about. even though the thought of that terrifies me, i would want people to get over me.

what would 'drive me to a decision'? i dont know. i have no reason. i can see no future, i dont want to work on things, i cant see working on myself, i cant see things working out. all the advice ive gotten is 'things will get better' or some backhanded advice that only makes me feel worse in the end.

ive been incredibly selfish with this post. im sorry.

this is not a note or a letter or anything its just saying how i feel.
